## ROOT CAUSE SECTION MOTION
`useInView` from `motion/react` was unreliable with SSR hydration timing. Replaced with native `IntersectionObserver` in `use-reveal.tsx` with `threshold: 0.05` and `rootMargin: "0px 0px -20px 0px"`. Added `hasAnimated` flag to prevent re-triggering. Increased translateY from 32px to 40px and duration from 0.7s to 0.8s for more obvious animation.
